Accuracy is an estimate, not a guarantee. Every failure path favors freezing or manual delay over revealing newer data.

## Camera design scenario

Camera mode is disabled in Phase 1. A future flow requires explicit permission, a user-selected scoreboard region, local sampling where practical, immediate stop, normalized clock/score retention only, and no raw-frame upload by default. OCR errors or ambiguous states lower confidence and cannot authorize a forward reveal by themselves.
